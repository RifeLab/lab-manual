# Letter of recommendation intake sheet for Trevor Rife (the letter writer)

This form was inspired by a resource created by Dr. Dana Miller and Dr. Christina Termini.

## Instructions
Please answer the following questions on this sheet.
Save your answers as a .docx file.
Please name the file “LastName_Year_LOR_Request” (i.e.: Rife_2023_LOR_Request).
Send this file along with your current CV (as a PDF file named “LastName_Year_CV”) to Trevor at least two weeks before the letter is due.
Please send a reminder email to Trevor the week the letter is due. 

I understand that it may not always be possible to provide this information to me two weeks before a letter is due – some opportunities come out of nowhere.
If I can provide a letter under a shorter timescale, I will, but this may not always be possible.

Please complete one of these forms for every opportunity you request a letter, even if the same letter may be adapted for each.
If you are completing this form to obtain a letter for graduate school or medical school applications, please complete the corresponding questions.
If not, please ignore these sections.

This questionnaire is intended to help me write the best-informed letter of recommendation for your consideration as an applicant for various opportunities.
Please keep this in mind when completing the form.
